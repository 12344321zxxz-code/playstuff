# Formicarium 2: the brief

Answers from Ariel, 8 October 2026. This replaces the direction in DESIGN.md where they disagree.

## What it is
- A sandbox with soft goals (the Almanac). No win, no ending; worlds run forever.
- You mostly **watch stories unfold**. Sessions of about an hour. Desktop.
- References: Dwarf Fortress legends, SimEarth / From Dust, WorldBox.

## World
- **One continent, roughly Europe-sized**, room for 10 to 20 peoples. Flat top-down.
- Start from a **template** (pangaea, archipelago, ice world, and so on). Save and load.
- Close in you see **buildings, fields and roads**. Time flows continuously.
- Balanced realism: plausible and readable.
- History moves in **long cycles**, driven mostly by **climate and ecology**.

## Look and sound
- **Old atlas with colour**: a hand-drawn feel with rich painted colours.
- What bothers him now: the UI and the colours.
- **Radial menu**, organised in rings by category (Land, Sky, Life, Wrath, Other world).
- **Sound matters**: folk and medieval music, world sounds by place and zoom, stings for
  events, effects for powers.
- **The look comes first** in the new build.

## The living world (central, not background)
- Food webs (plants, grazers, predators, scavengers), biomes that advance and retreat, and
  sea life.
- No designing species.
- Monsters belong to the ecosystem: lairs and raids, and named legendary beasts.
- Magic comes from the land (ley lines, wells, old forests).

## Peoples and story
- Races: **humans and beast folk** (no elves or dwarves).
- Gods exist only as faiths. Your own acts become myths that **found faiths about you**.
- **Key figures**: rulers, prophets, heroes and villains. You can **nudge them** (bless,
  curse, send a vision). Your other powers act on places, not people.
- Peoples reshape the world a lot: land use, water works, megastructures, climate impact.
- Stories show **equally on the map and in the book**. A full **legends browser** covers
  every people, figure, place, artifact and beast.
- Favourite moments: empires rising and falling, nature against people, lost and found.

## Powers
- Unlimited, no cost. He uses every current group: land, sky, wrath, life, other world.

## Build
- **Fresh build, done properly for the web**: TypeScript, a proper renderer, the simulation
  in a background thread, sound. It still opens as a page.
- Keep from v1: climate and terrain, the peoples simulation, the chronicle and almanac, and
  travellers.
- He does not want check-ins: ship it when it is good.
